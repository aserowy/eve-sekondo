Signal

An item is listed when its price fell unusually far and is expected to return to its average. The Bollinger bands are the SMA 20 of the daily averages ± 2 standard deviations of the last 20 daily averages. %B tells where the latest daily average sits in them: 0 on the lower band, 1 on the upper band, below 0 under the lower band. RSI 14 weighs the rises of the last 14 days against the falls, from 0 to 100. The item counts as oversold when %B is below 0 or RSI is below 30. S is the SMA 20 rounded down to a valid price, the average the price is expected to return to, and must be above B.

For example, with an SMA 20 of 10,537.40, %B −0.08 and RSI 27, the item is oversold and S is 10,530.00.
