Signal

An item is listed when its spread widens again and again at the same time: the window. With at least 7 days of own hourly snapshots, an hour of day recurs when, on at least 60% of the last 14 days with data for it (and on at least 7 days), its highest spread was at least 1.5 times the median hourly spread of 30 days. A weekday and hour recur when that held in 3 of the last 4 weeks. With fewer days of snapshots, only weekdays are looked at, from the daily figures. S is one tick below the 25th percentile of the best sell peaks of the past occurrences: a price most occurrences reached.

For example, the window “daily 18:00” peaked at 12,000, 12,200, 12,500, 12,600, 12,800, 13,000, 13,100, 13,300, 13,500 and 14,000. Their 25th percentile is 12,525.00, and one tick below it, S is 12,520.00.

Order playbook

  1. Place the buy order well before the window.
  2. Once it has filled, place the sell order at S before the next start shown in the row.
  3. When the sell order has not filled by the end of the window, wait for the next occurrence or re-check the row, instead of chasing the price down.
