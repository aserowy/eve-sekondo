Signal

An item is listed when its price has started to rise: the SMA 5 of the daily averages crossed above the SMA 20 within the last 3 days, and the average daily volume of the last 5 days is above that of the last 20, so the rise is traded. S is one tick below the current best sell, so your sell order is the cheapest one.

For example, the SMA5 crossed above the SMA20 two days ago, and 95,000 units a day traded over the last 5 days against 80,000 over 20. With a best sell of 11,800.00, S is 11,790.00.
