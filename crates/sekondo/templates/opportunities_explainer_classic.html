Signal

Every item with a best buy and a best sell is a candidate: the trade lives on the spread between them. S is one tick below the current best sell, so your sell order is the cheapest one. The item is listed when the shared figures and your filters let it through.

For example, with a best sell of 11,500.00, S is 11,490.00.

Order playbook

    {% include "opportunities_explainer_playbook_common.html" %}
  1. Place the sell order at S, one tick below the best sell, as soon as the buy order has filled.
  2. Watch both sides: being outbid on the buy side or undercut on the sell side stops the fills.
  3. Re-price by one tick only while m still clears your minimum margin.
